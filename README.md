## 🧭 A Brief Word Before You Wander In

There is a particular kind of quiet that settles over Elderfield when the sun dips behind the western granary — a hush where the wheat sways and the river hums and every farmer dreams of a slightly better tomorrow. **Elderfield Companion Suite** was born from that hush. It is not a sledgehammer. It is a set of well-oiled garden shears, a lantern, and a notebook — a companion for those who wish to explore the rolling meadows and shadowed crypts of Elderfield with a bit more grace, a bit more curiosity, and a great deal more convenience.

Where the original *Welcome to Elderfield modkit* offered thirty-seven thoughtful modules, this project takes the spirit further — **forty-two hand-polished utilities** spanning cultivation, combat, craftsmanship, ritual work, and the curious machinery of the world itself. Each one was designed with the same question in mind: *does this make the journey more enjoyable, or merely shorter?* If the answer was the latter, it was discarded.

This README is long because the suite is long. Pour a cup of tea. We will walk the whole field together.

## 🌱 What Is This, Really?

Imagine you are a steward of a small plot in Elderfield. You have crops to tend, a cellar to stock, neighbors to trade with, and — on the odd moonlit evening — a skeleton or two to politely discourage. Vanilla Elderfield asks you to do all of this with one hand tied behind your back. Not out of cruelty, of course; the designers wanted you to *feel* the weight of the land.

The **Elderfield Companion Suite** loosens that knot. It is a modular toolkit that layers on top of your existing game and offers optional improvements — a speedier harvest here, a smarter inventory there, a ritual assistant for the particularly arcane evenings. Every module can be toggled from the in-game panel, so your Elderfield can remain as harsh or as hospitable as you prefer.

Think of it as the difference between walking across a field and walking across a field with a good pair of boots. Same field. Same sky. Better journey.

### The Design Philosophy in Three Lines

1. **Preserve the soul, smooth the friction.** We do not rewrite Elderfield. We polish the rails it runs on.
2. **Everything is optional.** No module is forced. No setting is permanent. Change your mind freely, as often as you like.
3. **Respect the world.** Save data stays intact, guardrails are in place, and nothing is ever written that cannot be gracefully undone.

## ✨ The Grand Feature Ledger

The suite is organized into five families of modules. Each family addresses a different rhythm of play — the slow rhythm of the field, the sharp rhythm of combat, the patient rhythm of the workshop, the hushed rhythm of ritual, and the wanderer's rhythm of the open world.

### 🌾 Cultivation & Harvest Modules

For those who love the land more than the sword. Twelve modules live here, and each one treats the soil as something sacred.

- **🌱 Seedbed Sentinel** — Automatically tracks seed stocks and reminds you, gently, when a plot has sat fallow longer than it should.
- **🌾 Harvest Rhythm** — A configurable cadence for harvesting that respects your preferred pace. Choose a meditative slow mode or an efficient brisk mode; both are valid, and both are yours.
- **💧 Irrigation Whisper** — Monitors soil moisture across your holdings and paints a soft overlay showing which beds are thirsty.
- **🐝 Apiary Harmony** — Improves the odds of pollinator events without ever feeling unfair, and logs every visit for the curious apiarist.
- **🌻 Seasonal Loom** — Visibility into crop rotations and season transitions, so you can plan three harvests ahead.
- **🧺 Granary Ledger** — A tidy ledger of everything you have stored, sold, or gifted. Sortable, searchable, and exportable.
- **🍎 Orchard Companion** — Fruit trees bear with a touch more generosity and a touch more predictability.
- **🐄 Pasture Keeper** — Livestock happiness tracking with gentle nudges when an animal has been overlooked.
- **🌿 Forager's Filter** — Highlights harvestable wild plants in your immediate surroundings with a subtle, non-intrusive outline.
- **🪴 Greenhouse Flow** — Manages greenhouse climatology with sliders instead of guesswork.
- **🌰 Seed Vault** — Long-term seed preservation so heirloom varieties survive the cold months.
- **🍇 Vine Tender** — Specialized tuning for grape and vine crops, tuned for winemakers and dreamers alike.

### ⚔️ Combat & Fieldcraft Modules

Nine modules for the evenings when the fields are not enough.

- **🛡️ Guard Stance Coach** — A situational awareness overlay that highlights incoming threats in soft amber rather than alarm-red.
- **🗡️ Weapon Familiarity Tracker** — Logs every weapon you have wielded and how comfortable you have become with each. Knowledge is a kind of strength.
- **🏹 Quiver Efficiency** — Ammunition and arrow management that respects inventory weight and never overstuffs your pack.
- **🐺 Beast Behavior Atlas** — A bestiary that fills in as you observe creatures in the wild. No spoilers; only what you have witnessed.
- **🩹 Field Medic Notes** — Tracks the effectiveness of bandages, salves, and rest in your personal log.
- **🔥 Camp Discipline** — Camp quality indicators to help you choose the best spot for the night.
- **🌫️ Fog of War Whisper** — Configurable visibility so you can admire the scenery or plan your path without the sudden ambush.
- **⚖️ Encumbrance Fairness** — Softens the sharpest edges of the weight system while still honoring it.
- **🏕️ Retreat Logger** — Records every tactical retreat so you can study your patterns later. There is no shame in a wise withdrawal.

### 🔨 Crafting & Workshop Modules

Eight modules for the tinkerers, the blacksmiths, and the quiet souls who love the smell of sawdust.

- **🔧 Recipe Sight** — Reveals recipe combinations you have already discovered, in a beautiful illustrated grid.
- **⚒️ Anvil Cadence** — A rhythm-keeper for smithing minigames that respects your hand-eye preferences.
- **🧵 Loom Patience** — Weaving and tailoring assistance that never hurries you.
- **🪚 Workshop Planner** — Queue up crafting tasks and let the suite handle the sequencing.
- **🪙 Material Counter** — Real-time tally of every component you own, with notes on what each is for.
- **🧴 Alchemy Notes** — A personal journal of every potion and mixture you have brewed, including near-misses.
- **🖋️ Enchantment Workbook** — Records every enchantment you have applied, and gently reminds you of compatible combinations.
- **🔮 Artificer's Blueprint** — Long-term planning for complex constructs, spread across multiple sessions.

### 🕯️ Ritual & Arcana Modules

Seven modules for the hour between midnight and dawn, when the world leans in to listen.

- **🕯️ Candle Arrangement** — Assists with ritual circle layouts, paying attention to aesthetics and symbolic correctness.
- **📖 Grimoire Index** — A searchable index of every incantation, sigil, and phrase you have collected.
- **🌙 Lunar Almanac** — Tracks moon phases, tides, and celestial alignments relevant to your rituals.
- **🔮 Scrying Clarity** — Sharpens scrying visions so you can read them without squinting.
- **🕸️ Binding Thread Map** — Visualizes spiritual and magical connections between locations and entities.
- **🪬 Ward Monitor** — Alerts you when a ward you placed has been disturbed, without panic or alarm.
- **🌌 Astral Log** — A dream journal for astral journeys, kept safe and encrypted at rest.

### 🗺️ World Utility Modules

Six modules for wanderers, cartographers, and the incurably curious.

- **🗺️ Pathfinder's Notebook** — Annotate the map with your own markers, notes, and sketches.
- **🧭 Waypoint Weaver** — Create custom waypoints with names, colors, and descriptions.
- **🌤️ Weather Sense** — A three-day forecast with confidence intervals, because meteorology should be an art *and* a science.
- **⏳ Chrono Ledger** — Track in-game time spent on each activity so you can see where your seasons go.
- **📯 Courier Beacon** — Manage messages and deliveries with a neat inbox that never overflows.
- **🎼 Ambient Log** — Notes on the sounds, songs, and silences you have encountered — a personal soundtrack of Elderfield.

## 🎨 The Interface, Reimagined

The panel was not designed in a hurry. Every control has been placed with the same care a farmer places fence posts — straight, spaced well, and built to last many seasons. Highlights include:

- **Responsive Layout** — From a 13-inch laptop to a widescreen monitor, the panel reflows gracefully without ever clipping a button or hiding a slider.
- **Dark, Dawn & Daylight Themes** — Three carefully tuned palettes so that the interface never fights the game's own mood.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation** — Every function is reachable without a mouse, for the players who prefer their hands on the keys.
- **Gesture Shortcuts** — Optional swipe and drag gestures for those who enjoy a tactile interface.
- **Live Search** — A search bar that finds any module, setting, or note in under a keystroke.
- **Accessibility Built In** — Adjustable font sizes, high-contrast modes, and full screen-reader support where the platform allows it.
- **Instant Preview Mode** — Toggle a setting and see its effect in real time, without closing the panel.

## 🛡️ Stability, Safety & Stewardship

A companion should never endanger the traveler. Every design decision in this suite has been made with that principle in mind.

- **Save Integrity First** — Every write to your save is journaled and reversible. If anything goes sideways, one command restores the previous state.
- **No Silent Behavior** — Every module announces itself in the log. Nothing happens that you did not ask for.
- **Config Backups** — Settings are snapshotted on every launch, so a corrupted config never costs you more than one session.
- **Sandboxed Telemetry** — We collect *nothing*. No metrics, no analytics, no phone-home. Your Elderfield is your own.
- **Open Audit Trail** — Every release ships with a changelog, and every change is traceable to a pull request.
- **Compatibility Patches** — Regular checks against the current game build, so you are warned well before a shift breaks anything.

## 📦 Getting the Suite Into Your Hands

The recommended path is to use the in-game installer included with each release, which handles all placement, verification, and conflict checks automatically. The manual path — for the curious and the tinkerers — is documented in the *Advanced Setup* section of the Knowledge Base.

A brief overview of the journey:

1. **Check compatibility.** Confirm your platform and game version against the release notes.
2. **Download the suite.** The download link is provided at the top of this document.
3. **Extract to a scratch folder.** Anywhere you can find again will do.
4. **Run the included launcher.** It will detect your Elderfield installation and offer to place the files for you.
5. **Launch the game.** The companion panel appears on the pause screen. Everything else is up to you.

If at any point you feel uncertain, stop. Support is one message away, and there is no such thing as a silly question.

## 🧪 Frequently Asked Curiosities

**Is this affiliated with the original modkit?**
No. This is an independent project inspired by the same beloved game. We hold the original team in high regard and encourage you to try both.

**Will this change my game permanently?**
Never. Every change is optional, toggleable, and reversible. Your vanilla experience is one click away at all times.

**Does it work on Steam Deck and similar handhelds?**
Yes. The interface detects small screens and adjusts controls accordingly.

**Can I disable only one module and keep the rest?**
Of course. Modules are independent, and disabling one has no effect on the others.

**How often is the suite updated?**
Roughly every two to four weeks, or sooner if the game receives a major update.

**Do you collect any data about me?**
No. Not a byte. Your play stays entirely yours.

**I want to contribute. Where do I start?**
Read the *Contributing* guide in the Knowledge Base, then pick up any issue labeled *good first step*. We will be glad to have you.
